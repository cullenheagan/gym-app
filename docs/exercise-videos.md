# Exercise demo videos (My plan)

Every exercise My plan can suggest needs one demo video (HCI proposal F4: 100% coverage). Find a video for each row, paste the YouTube link in the last column, and send the list back (or edit `video:` for that id in `src/lib/exercise-library.ts`). `npm test` lists any that are still missing.

**What makes a good video**
- Short: ideally under 2 minutes, or a link that starts at the demo (`&t=30s`).
- Shows the whole movement from the side or at an angle, at a normal speed, more than once.
- Made for beginners by a credible coach or physio channel, with the common mistakes mentioned.
- Has captions, or clear on-screen text (the proposal's accessibility requirement).
- Matches the version in the app (for example a goblet squat with one dumbbell held at the chest, not a barbell squat).
- Allows embedding: open the video, choose Share > Embed; if YouTube says embedding is disabled, pick another.
